**# Study Hour Counter 🌙**



**Study Hour Counter is a simple and easy-to-use tool designed for focused study sessions.**



**## How to use it**



**1. \*\*Set your goal\*\***  

&#x20;  **Choose the number of hours you want to study for your session and click \*\*CHECK\*\* to start.**



**2. \*\*Stay focused\*\***  

&#x20;  **A countdown timer will track your session. Every time an hour passes, you'll receive an alert and your progress will automatically update.**



**3. \*\*Finish whenever you need\*\***  

&#x20;  **If you decide to stop studying before the timer ends, simply click \*\*Finished\*\*. Your study time will be saved to your progress for the day.**



**4. \*\*Keep studying\*\***  

&#x20;  **Your study day isn't limited to one session. Once you've finished a session, you can set a new goal and start another one. Your sessions will continue accumulating in your daily progress.**



**5. \*\*Reach your goal\*\***  

&#x20;  **When your selected study time is complete, you'll receive a notification congratulating you on completing your session. 🎉**
